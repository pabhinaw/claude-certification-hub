# Question Bank & Practice <span class="level-badge">L4</span>

**214 original scenario-style questions** (12 of them multiple-response), organised by domain. Every answer explains why the correct option is right **and why each wrong option is wrong**. That second part is where most of the learning happens.

!!! note "Original practice material"
    All questions are written from public documentation and design principles. They are **not** real or recalled exam questions, and all names and data are fictional. See the [content policy](../resources/content-policy.md).

## How to get the most from it

1. **Answer before you look.** Commit to an option, then open the explanation.
2. **Read every "why the others are wrong" note**, even when you were right. Exam distractors repeat the same patterns.
3. **Track your misses by topic**, not by question. Then reread that topic on its domain page.
4. **Use the mocks last.** Do them under time pressure once your domain scores are consistently above 85%.

## Score guide (study mode)

| Your accuracy | What it suggests |
|---|---|
| 85%+ consistently | Ready for timed mocks |
| 70–84% | Revisit the domain pages for your weakest topics |
| under 70% | Work through the domain pages and [labs](../labs/index.md) first |

Practice percentages are a guide only. The real exam uses a scaled score, so practice results can't be converted into an exam score.
