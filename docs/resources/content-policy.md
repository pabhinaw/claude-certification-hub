# Content & Exam-Integrity Policy

This hub exists to help people **learn the skills** behind the Claude certifications. It is not a shortcut around the exam. These rules apply to every page and every contribution.

## What this site is

* An **independent, unofficial** community study resource.
* Built from **public documentation**, hands-on experience and original writing.
* **Not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Anthropic.**

## What this site will never contain

* **Real exam questions**, recalled questions, or descriptions of specific exam items. Anthropic's certification policy states that exam content is confidential and that candidates agree not to share, reproduce or discuss the questions in any form, including in study groups and online forums.
* **Copies of confidential materials**, such as the candidate exam guide or score-report wording.
* **Anthropic logos or brand assets.** "Claude" and "Anthropic" appear only as plain-text names, to say what the content is about.
* **Personal or real customer data.** All examples use fictional test data.
* **Claims of official status**, guaranteed pass rates, or "real exam" or "dump" style marketing.

## How content is written

* **Practice questions are original.** They are written from public documentation and design principles, not reconstructed from memory of any exam.
* **Technical facts link to official documentation** wherever practical, and pages carry a review date.
* **Exam logistics** (format, price, eligibility) come from public pages and may change. Always confirm with Anthropic before you book.

## Reporting a problem

If you believe any page breaks these rules, or a fact is out of date, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/pabhinaw/claude-certification-hub/issues). It will be reviewed and corrected promptly.
