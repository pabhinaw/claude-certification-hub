## What this site is

It's free, independent and unofficial. A few rules I stick to:

* **Nothing comes from the real exams.** Every practice question is written from scratch.
* **Facts that change link to the source.** Products move quickly, so for anything like a limit, a price or a parameter, Anthropic's documentation is the authority.
* **Examples use made-up data**, never real people or companies.
* **If a beginner can't follow it, I rewrite it.**
